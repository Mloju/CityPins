 CITY-PINS

AI-Powered Local Discovery & Social Media Analytics

Project Overview

City-Pins is an AI-powered social media analytics platform designed to help people discover places, events, and local trends in their city. The project will analyze public Reddit conversations to identify cafés, restaurants, study spots, events, and other locations that people are discussing and recommending.

The results will be presented as interactive “pins” on a city map, allowing users to explore what people are currently talking about and discover places based on social media activity, sentiment, popularity, and relevance.

Problem Statement

Finding good local places and events often requires searching through multiple websites and social media platforms. It can be difficult to determine what places are currently popular, what people actually think about them, and which new places or events are becoming popular.

City-Pins aims to answer the question:

What are people discovering, recommending, and talking about in their city right now?

By analyzing social media conversations, the project will turn large amounts of unstructured information into useful and easy-to-understand local insights.

Objectives

The main objectives of City-Pins are to:

* Collect and analyze public Reddit posts and comments related to local places and events.
* Identify sentiment, popular topics, and emerging local trends.
* Identify locations and events that receive significant attention or engagement.
* Develop recommendations based on social media data, relevance, sentiment, and engagement.
* Create an interactive map and dashboard for exploring local insights.
* Evaluate the limitations, bias, privacy, and ethical concerns associated with using social media data.

Research Questions

The project will focus on the following research questions:

1. What places and events are most frequently discussed in local social media communities?
2. What factors contribute to positive or negative sentiment toward local places and events?
3. Which places, events, or topics show signs of emerging popularity?
4. Can social media data be used to provide relevant recommendations for local places and events?

Data Sources and Analytics

The primary data source will be public Reddit posts and comments from relevant city and community subreddits. Where appropriate, open municipal datasets may also be used to provide additional information about locations and events.

The project will use several analytics techniques, including:

* Sentiment Analysis: Analyze opinions and identify positive, negative, and neutral discussions.
* NLP & Entity Extraction: Identify places, events, topics, and other important information from text.
* Recommendation System: Recommend places or events using factors such as relevance, sentiment, engagement, and recency.

Proposed Workflow

The project will follow an end-to-end analytics workflow:

Reddit / Open Data → Data Cleaning & Preprocessing → NLP & Embeddings → Sentiment & Entity Extraction → Trend / Network Analysis → Recommendations → City-Pins Dashboard**

The data will first be collected and cleaned before being analyzed using NLP and machine learning techniques. The results will then be combined to identify trends and generate recommendations that can be displayed through the dashboard.

Final Product

The final product will be an interactive Streamlit dashboard featuring a city map with virtual pins representing relevant places and events.

Users will be able to:

* Explore popular and trending locations.
* View sentiment surrounding places and events.
* Search for topics or interests.
* Explore local discussions and trends.
* Receive recommendations based on analyzed social media data.

Ethics and Privacy

The project will use appropriate publicly available data and avoid unnecessary collection or use of personally identifying information. Since Reddit users do not represent the entire population, the project will acknowledge potential sampling and demographic biases.

The analysis will also consider risks related to misinformation, privacy, biased recommendations, and the overrepresentation of highly active online communities. These limitations will be clearly communicated when presenting the results.

Expected Outcome

City-Pins will demonstrate an end-to-end social media analytics solution that transforms public online conversations into useful local insights. The project will combine NLP, machine learning, sentiment analysis, trend detection, recommendation techniques, and interactive visualization to help users better understand what is happening and becoming popular in their city.
