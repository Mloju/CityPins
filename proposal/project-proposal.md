CITY-PINS
AI-Powered Local Discovery & Social Media Analytics

Project Overview
City-Pins is an AI-powered social media analytics platform designed to help people discover places, events, and local trends in their city. The project will analyze public conversations from multiple social media platforms, primarily Reddit and YouTube, to identify cafés, restaurants, study spots, events, and other locations that people are discussing and recommending.
The results will be presented as interactive “pins” on a city map, allowing users to explore what people are currently talking about and discover places based on social media activity, sentiment, popularity, and relevance.

Problem Statement
Finding good local places and events often requires searching through multiple websites and social media platforms. It can be difficult to determine what places are currently popular, what people think about them, and which new places or events are becoming popular.
City-Pins aims to answer: “What are people discovering, recommending, and talking about in their city right now?”
By analyzing conversations across multiple platforms, the project will transform large amounts of unstructured social media information into useful and easy-to-understand local insights.

Objectives
• Collect and analyze public social media conversations related to local places and events. 
• Integrate data from Reddit and YouTube with relevant open municipal data where appropriate. 
• Identify sentiment, popular topics, and emerging local trends. 
• Identify locations and events that receive significant attention or engagement. 
• Develop recommendations based on social media data, relevance, sentiment, engagement, and recency. 
• Create an interactive map and dashboard for exploring local insights.
• Evaluate limitations, bias, privacy, and ethical concerns associated with social media data.

Research Questions
1. What places and events are most frequently discussed across Reddit and YouTube? 
2. What factors contribute to positive or negative sentiment toward local places and events?
3. Which places, events, or topics show signs of emerging popularity? 
4. Can social media data be used to provide relevant recommendations for local places and events?

Data Sources and Analytics
Data Sources
The project will use a multi-platform dataset consisting primarily of: 
• Reddit: Public posts and comments from relevant city and community subreddits. 
• YouTube: Public videos, comments, and engagement information related to local places, events, and topics. 
• Open Municipal Data: Where available, public datasets may supplement location, event, and geographic information e.g City of Ottawa 

The team will identify appropriate data sources and begin collection during the data collection phase of the project.
Analytics Techniques
• Sentiment Analysis: Analyze opinions and identify positive, negative, and neutral discussions. 
• NLP & Entity Extraction: Identify places, events, topics, and other relevant information from text. 
• Trend Analytics: Identify changes in discussion volume, engagement, and emerging topics. 
• Network Analysis: Explore relationships between communities, topics, and locations. 
• Recommendation System: Recommend places or events using relevance, sentiment, engagement, and recency.

Proposed Workflow
Reddit + YouTube + Open Data → Data Collection & Integration → Cleaning & Preprocessing → NLP & Embeddings → Sentiment & Entity/Topic Extraction → Trend & Network Analysis → Recommendation System → City-Pins Dashboard

Final Product
The final product will be an interactive Streamlit dashboard featuring a city map with virtual pins representing relevant places and events.
Users will be able to: 
• Explore popular and trending locations. 
• View sentiment surrounding places and events.
• Search for topics or interests. 
• Explore local discussions and trends. 
• Receive recommendations based on analyzed social media data.

Ethics and Privacy
The project will use appropriate publicly available data and avoid unnecessary collection or use of personally identifying information.
Because social media users do not represent the entire population, the project will acknowledge potential sampling and demographic biases. The analysis will also consider risks related to misinformation, privacy, biased recommendations, and the overrepresentation of highly active online communities.
The limitations of social-media-based insights will be clearly communicated when presenting the results.
